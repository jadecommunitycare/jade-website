Why Jade

A small agency, on purpose.

You will not be a badge number here. Our homes are small, our schedules are built with the staff who work them, and when you call the office someone picks up.

Most of our direct care staff came in with no experience in intellectual and developmental disabilities. We train you, we pay for the certifications the state requires, and we promote from within — several of our home managers started as overnight staff.

  • Paid training and certificationCPR, First Aid, and required HCS training at no cost to you

  • Overtime availablePick up extra shifts when you want them

Open Positions

Where we need people right now

All positions are based in Richmond, Texas, and serve our homes and day program throughout Fort Bend County and the surrounding areas.

Registered Nurse (RN)

Full-time · Part-time · PRN

Richmond, TX · Travel between homes required

Our RNs are the clinical backbone of the agency. You will assess the people we serve, write and update nursing care plans, train and delegate to direct care staff, oversee medication administration, and respond when something changes.

  • Current, unencumbered Texas RN license (or compact license valid in Texas)
  • Experience with intellectual and developmental disabilities preferred — not required
  • Comfortable working independently across multiple homes
  • Valid Texas driver's license, reliable vehicle, and current auto insurance
  • Able to pass a criminal background check, Employee Misconduct Registry check, and Nurse Aide Registry check

ISS / Day Habilitation Staff

Full-time · Part-time · Weekdays

Richmond, TX · On-site, out in the community, and in-home

Our day program is growing, and this is the job at the center of it. You support a small group of adults through their day — running activities at our licensed site, getting out into the community, and working on the specific goals in each person's plan. Then you write down what happened and what progress looked like.

  • 18 years or older with a high school diploma or GED
  • No prior experience required — we train you
  • Comfortable planning and leading activities, and adapting when they do not go to plan
  • Willing to drive the people we serve to community outings
  • Valid Texas driver's license, reliable vehicle, and current auto insurance
  • Able to pass a criminal background check and Employee Misconduct Registry check
  • Patient, creative, and genuinely interested in what someone else wants out of their day

Direct Care Staff / Direct Support Professional

Full-time · Part-time · Weekends

Richmond, TX · Group homes · Day, evening, and overnight shifts

This is the job at the center of everything we do. You help the people in one of our homes with daily living — personal care, meals, medication reminders, appointments, errands, and getting out into the community — and you document what happened on each shift.

  • 18 years or older with a high school diploma or GED
  • No prior experience required — we train you
  • Valid Texas driver's license, reliable vehicle, and current auto insurance
  • Able to lift and physically assist an adult, and to pass a TB screening
  • Able to pass a criminal background check and Employee Misconduct Registry check
  • Patient, dependable, and genuinely willing to show up for someone

Overnight Direct Care Staff

Overnight · Full-time · Part-time

Richmond, TX · Awake overnight shifts

Awake overnight coverage in our group homes: safety checks, assistance during the night, morning routines, household tasks, and documentation. A good fit if you want steady hours and a calmer pace.

  • Same qualifications as direct care staff, plus the ability to stay awake and alert overnight
  • Reliability matters more here than anywhere else on the schedule

Host Home Provider

Contracted · In your own home

Fort Bend County, Greater Houston, and surrounding areas

Host home providers welcome one person into their own home and provide day-to-day support as part of the family. Jade screens, trains, monitors, and pays you, and our staff stay involved throughout.

  • A safe, stable home with space for one additional adult
  • Willing to complete home study, training, and ongoing monitoring visits
  • All household members 18 and over must pass background and registry checks
  • Patience, flexibility, and a real willingness to share your life with someone

What to Expect

Our hiring process

  1. You apply online

    About ten minutes. You can attach a resume, but you do not need one.

  2. We call you

    Usually within two to three business days. A short conversation about the role, your availability, and what you are looking for.

  3. Interview

    In person at our Richmond office, or at the home you would be working in.

  4. Paperwork, background checks, and onboarding

    If we make you an offer, this is where the rest of the forms come in — background check and registry authorizations, I-9 and W-4, a TB screening, and Jade's policy acknowledgements. We hand you the packet in person and go through it with you. Then paid training before your first solo shift.
